# Limitations

- The physics is restricted to static leading-eikonal/geodesic formulas.
- Synthetic data test inference only inside the assumed analytic families.
- The damped waveform is illustrative and not a calibrated detector waveform.
- PCA curves cannot add information beyond the quantities used to generate them.
- Geodesic-observable values are synthetic proxies unless replaced by validated
  direct/secondary shooting outputs.
- The GW150914 branch propagates a GR remnant posterior and does not fit strain.
- The Kerr fallback is an approximate dominant-mode fit when `qnm` is unavailable.
- Detector covariance, calibration uncertainty, selection effects, and a non-GR
  likelihood are not included.
- Results must not be described as modified-gravity constraints or hair detection.
