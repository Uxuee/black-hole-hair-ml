# CQG submission-format checklist

Verified current guidance permits journal-neutral initial submissions and requests one readable PDF with figures and tables placed in the text. For final packaging, check:

- manuscript source and compiled PDF;
- title page, author names, affiliations, and corresponding-author details;
- abstract and any required keywords;
- Data Availability statement;
- complete references and final JCAP metadata;
- publication-quality figure files and captions;
- supplementary/reproducibility files and publisher size limits;
- cover letter;
- any requested suggested referees or exclusions.

The scientific master remains one-column and journal-neutral. No unsupported CQG house-style conversion was made.
