# Standard-model choice rationale

The objective is not to maximize a benchmark score with a highly tuned architecture, but to determine which conclusions reflect the physical inverse map rather than one estimator. HGB and RF expose the piecewise behavior of tree ensembles, while the MLP provides a smoother perturbation response; their differing extrapolation failures are therefore informative. The frozen-estimator robustness audit further separates sensitivity to feature resolution from retraining effects. These familiar model families are intentional heterogeneous probes, not a claim that standard models are universally preferable.
