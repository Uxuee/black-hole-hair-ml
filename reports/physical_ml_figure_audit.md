# Audit of physical/ML manuscript figures

## Scope and result

Figures 6--11 in `paper/ai4s2026/main.tex` were traced back to the committed
machine-readable results and regenerated without refitting models or changing
scientific outputs. The audit passes all figure/table consistency checks. It
also confirms the previously reported high-resolution robustness failure; the
new presentation makes that limitation more explicit rather than hiding it.

## Figure 8 aggregation error

The old plotting expression selected `metric == "NMAE"` and then called a
default `pivot_table(..., values="median")`. The default aggregation was an
arithmetic **mean of row medians**, pooled over both targets, all three model
families, and (for extrapolation) all four directions. It did not sum
directions, use raw absolute errors, or include unscored `k=0` values in the
ordinary `wq` metric. Four extreme directional MLP cells had median NMAE above
1 (the maximum was 15.4257); their inclusion in the arithmetic mean produced
the misleading 1.1020 and 1.2332 bars for all-shooting and ringdown-plus-all-
shooting. These are poor MLP extrapolation outcomes, not records marked as
software failures, so they remain in the source table and in dispersion.

The corrected figure separates `k` from identifiable `wq`. For each protocol,
feature set, and target, its central point is the median of the model-level
source medians (and direction/model medians for extrapolation), exactly as in
Table V. IQR bars are calculated from `fold_metrics.csv` and retain fold, seed,
model, and direction dispersion. A separate archived figure shows all four
directions.

| Ringdown | 0.2118 | 0.0796 | 0.3011 |

## Table V consistency

Table V now reports Random, Grouped, and Directional median NMAE for both
targets. Its 30 entries are generated by the same explicit grouping rule used
by Figure 8. `tests/test_physical_ml_manuscript_figures.py` asserts all 30
full-precision central values, verifies complete feature/target coverage, and
checks that the exact `k=0` boundary is zero while all mapped interior values
are finite and positive.

The corrected directional values span 0.0796--0.2305 for `k` and
0.2012--0.3011 for identifiable `wq`, consistent with the manuscript text and
unlike the old pooled bars. Random and grouped values are unchanged.

## Caption audit

Every corrected axis and caption now names the statistic it displays. Figure 9
explicitly marks higher `sigma_min` as better and lower condition number/NMAE
as better, while its caption limits the complementarity claim: physical
conditioning generally agrees with tree-based grouped `wq` performance but
does not perfectly rank every model, target, or protocol. Figure 11 now
distinguishes maximum individual prediction shift from absolute aggregate NMAE
change; its previous caption described only the latter despite the underlying
audit table containing both.

## Unresolved concerns

- The high-resolution inverse-model robustness criterion remains failed:
  maximum raw prediction shifts are approximately 0.000614 in `k` and 0.100 in
  `wq`, and the maximum absolute aggregate NMAE change is approximately 0.093.
- Directional MLP extrapolation has four extreme cells above NMAE 1. They are
  valid recorded outcomes rather than silent failures; robust medians and the
  separate direction plot prevent them from dominating the central summary.
- Conformal extrapolation intervals have no exchangeability guarantee. The
  shown coverage is descriptive for the tested directional shifts.
- The figure audit changes scientific communication and aggregation only. It
  does not establish robustness of the inverse model to phase resolution.
